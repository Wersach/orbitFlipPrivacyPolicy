Privacy Policy

Orbit Flip by Eretryx · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how the Orbit Flip game for Android handles information. If you have any questions, write to eretryx@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Orbit Flip has no accounts, sign-in, chat or in-app purchases, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, files or location.

Your progress and settings (scores, level, achievements, sound and graphics options) are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads. Ads are provided by Yandex through the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, which includes AppMetrica. To show ads and count how they perform, these services may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • basic device and app information, such as device model, OS version, language, network type and IP address;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped.

Yandex processes this information under its own privacy policy. The game does not ask for location permission. Data sent to Yandex is encrypted.

Your choices

You can turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads). Uninstalling the game stops all data collection by the game and its ad services.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any information. The only third party involved is Yandex, as described above.

Children

Orbit Flip is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the game and on this page.
